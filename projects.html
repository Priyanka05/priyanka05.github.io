Research & Projects

A closer look at architectural ideas and systems research across energy harvesting, data structures and shared-cache coherence.

Research & Projects

  1. OSCAR: Optimizing Shared CAches by Simplifying CoheRence [2026]

    Explores simplifications to shared-cache coherence behaviour to improve performance while avoiding additional storage overhead. Evaluated using gem5 Ruby and presented at CASES, ESWEEK 2026.

  2. CmpctArch: A Generic Low Power Architecture for Compact Data Structures in Energy Harvesting Devices [2022]

    Proposes a generic hardware architecture for implementing compact data structures on energy-harvesting devices. The approach reduces memory footprint by up to 3.5× while keeping energy and execution-time overhead low; hardware implementations are substantially more energy-efficient and faster than software implementations.

  3. A Survey on Checkpointing Techniques in Intermittent Systems [2021]

    A theoretical and experimental comparison of checkpointing approaches for energy-harvesting systems. A related FAQ document is available here.

  4. EHDSktch: A Generic Low Power Architecture for Sketching in Energy Harvesting Devices [2020]

    Proposes hardware IP blocks for implementing popular sketching algorithms in resource-constrained energy-harvesting devices, reducing energy consumption and improving performance over software implementations.

  5. FlexiCheck: An Adaptive Checkpointing Architecture for Energy Harvesting Devices [2019]

    Develops a formal model and checkpointing schedule for energy-harvesting systems, using optimization to identify efficient checkpointing policies.

  6. Scalable and Socially Consistent Systems [2018]

    Explores handling overload in social-networking systems through splitting and merging objects to reduce hot spots and improve scalability.

Other Past Projects
