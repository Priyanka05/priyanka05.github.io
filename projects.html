Research & Projects

A closer look at architectural ideas and systems research across energy harvesting, data structures and shared-cache coherence.

Research & Projects

  1. OSCAR: Optimizing Shared CAches by Simplifying CoheRence [2026]

    Explores simplifications to shared-cache coherence behaviour to improve performance while avoiding additional storage overhead. Evaluated using gem5 Ruby and presented at CASES, ESWEEK 2026.

  2. PERCH: Prefetch Aware Request Arbitration at Shared Caches [2026]

    Studies prefetch-aware request arbitration in shared caches. Accepted in ACM Transactions on Embedded Computing Systems, 2026.

  3. FARRE: Fairness Aware Request Response Arbitration in Shared Caches [2025]

    Explores fairness-aware request/response arbitration in shared caches. Presented at ESWEEK 2025 in Taipei, Taiwan, and published in ACM Transactions on Embedded Computing Systems, 2025.

  4. A Journey through Snoop Storm in Server SoCs [2024]

    An industry conference contribution on snoop-storm behaviour in server SoCs, presented at Intel’s DTTC 2024 conference.

  5. CmpctArch: A Generic Low Power Architecture for Compact Data Structures in Energy Harvesting Devices [2022]

    Proposes a generic hardware architecture for implementing compact data structures on energy-harvesting devices. The approach reduces memory footprint by up to 3.5× while keeping energy and execution-time overhead low; hardware implementations are substantially more energy-efficient and faster than software implementations.

  6. A Survey on Checkpointing Techniques in Intermittent Systems [2021]

    A theoretical and experimental comparison of checkpointing approaches for energy-harvesting systems. A related FAQ document is available here.

  7. EHDSktch: A Generic Low Power Architecture for Sketching in Energy Harvesting Devices [2020]

    Proposes hardware IP blocks for implementing popular sketching algorithms in resource-constrained energy-harvesting devices, reducing energy consumption and improving performance over software implementations.

  8. FlexiCheck: An Adaptive Checkpointing Architecture for Energy Harvesting Devices [2019]

    Develops a formal model and checkpointing schedule for energy-harvesting systems, using optimization to identify efficient checkpointing policies.

  9. Scalable and Socially Consistent Systems [2018]

    Explores handling overload in social-networking systems through splitting and merging objects to reduce hot spots and improve scalability.

Other Past Projects

  • AppSche: Intelligent Scheduler for Approximate Tasks on EHDs (2019). Developed a scheduling algorithm for approximate tasks on energy-harvesting devices, evaluated using the Tejas cycle-accurate simulator.
  • ReXplore — Explore as you research (2019). A PHP-based research collaboration platform with profile management, event calendars, publication repository and recommendations.
