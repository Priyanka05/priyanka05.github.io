Priyanka Singla

Caches & Coherence · NoCs & On-chip Fabrics · Chiplet Interconnects

I hold an M.E. from IISc Bangalore and a Ph.D. from IIT Delhi. After doctoral research in energy-harvesting systems and hardware–software co-design, I worked as a SoC Performance Architect at Intel. I am now a Performance Architect at Baya Systems, working on fabric and chiplet-system performance, shared caches, coherence and interconnects.

02 / RESEARCH

Questions I like working on

My work has evolved from energy-efficient embedded systems to cache and interconnect performance. The common thread is understanding bottlenecks and finding elegant architectural solutions.

01

Cache & memory systems

Shared caches, coherence protocols, memory hierarchy behaviour and performance trade-offs.

02

Interconnects & chiplets

On-chip fabrics, data movement, bandwidth, latency, congestion and scalable communication.

03

Energy-efficient architectures

Hardware–software co-design, energy harvesting, compact data structures and intermittent systems.

Mentoring & teaching: I have mentored undergraduate projects in computer architecture, simulation, automotive systems and energy harvesting, and assisted with courses in computer architecture, operating systems and digital logic.
